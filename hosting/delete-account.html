Delete your ScholAI account

This page is the way to ask ScholAI to delete the account created by the ScholAI app. You do not need the app installed to send the request.

Email amicablembakara50@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my ScholAI account”.

Include the Google email shown in ScholAI Settings. If you never linked Google, include the UID prefix shown on that same screen, and the approximate date you installed the app.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

Within 30 days we delete the Firebase account and the Cloud Firestore data tied to it: the profile, notification tokens, support requests and their messages, a professional application, and a professional listing if one was created for you.

Habit logs and other data that stayed on the phone are removed when you uninstall ScholAI or clear the app’s storage. Journal text and live audio already sent to Google Gemini stay under Google’s API terms. We cannot delete those copies from Google.
