# ScholAI — Brand Identity & Design System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Design Phase:** Concept & Brand Guidelines  
**Brand Pillars:** Warm, Grounded, Culturally Resonant, Quietly Intriguing  

---

## 1. Brand Concept: "The Sculptural Ember Ribbon" (Hybrid 2 — Approved)

ScholAI’s visual emblem is officially locked as **The Sculptural Ember Ribbon**:

### Visual Symbolism
1. **The Sculptural 'S' Ribbon**: Interlocking, fluid terracotta curves forming an organic **S** representing the student's inner journey, continuity, and personal reflection.
2. **The Inner Ember**: A glowing warm ember cradled in the center of the ribbon, symbolizing inner warmth, resilience, and keeping curiosity alive under academic pressure.
3. **Harmonic Negative Space**: An organic pocket inside the curve signifying breathing room and calm sanctuary.
4. **App Icon Tile**: Rendered on a deep midnight charcoal gradient background (`#1C1612` to `#251B15`) with subtle luminescence.

## 3. Colour Tokens & Architecture

Inspired by natural Nigerian terracotta pottery, warm earth tones, and midnight sanctuary dark mode.

### 3.1 Primary & Ambient
- **Primary Terracotta**: `#B85C38` (Brand mark, primary buttons, selected tabs)
- **Primary Light (Ember)**: `#D4845A` (Interactive hover/press, accent highlights)
- **Deep Ochre / Amber**: `#D4A853` (Warm notifications, middle mood tier)
- **Sage / Muted Olive**: `#7A8C5C` (Privacy badges, growth metrics, success states)

### 3.2 Canvas & Surfaces (AMOLED Dark Mode First)
- **Background Base**: `#1C1612` (Midnight Warm Charcoal)
- **Surface Mid**: `#2A201A` (Navigation bar, bottom sheets)
- **Surface Card**: `#332820` (Chat bubbles, mood selectors, cards)
- **Border / Divider**: `rgba(245, 235, 224, 0.08)`

### 3.3 Text & Hierarchy
- **Text High Emphasis**: `#F5EBE0` (Soft Cream — primary headings and text)
- **Text Medium Emphasis**: `#B0907A` (Warm Sand — subtitles and placeholder copy)
- **Text Muted**: `#6B4E3A` (Deep Earth — inactive labels, borders)

### 3.4 Mood Scale Gradient (5 Tiers)
1. **Rough**: `#8B3A3A` (Deep Red-Earth)
2. **Low**: `#B87333` (Raw Copper)
3. **Okay**: `#D4A853` (Warm Amber)
4. **Good**: `#7A9E6A` (Muted Leaf Green)
5. **Great**: `#4E8B6F` (Deep Forest Green)

## 4. UI Design Rules & Micro-Interactions

1. **Rounded Radii**:
   - Small components (chips, badges): `12px`
   - Medium components (buttons, input fields): `14px`
   - Large cards & chat containers: `16px - 20px`
2. **Tactile Feedback**:
   - Mood selector animates on tap with a gentle scale pop (`1.05x`) and soft glow border.
3. **"Quiet" Sanctuary Aesthetic**:
   - Generous breathing space (minimum 16px–24px outer padding).
   - No flashy casino-like streaks or confetti celebrations.
4. **Voice & Tone for "Scholly"**:
   - Empathetic, grounded, concise.
   - Socratic: asks questions to foster self-awareness rather than prescribing advice.
